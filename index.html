Freeze Your Credit

Freeze your credit at all three bureaus.

Each one is separate.

A credit freeze stops lenders from seeing your credit report, which makes it much harder for anyone to open an account in your name. Freezing and unfreezing are free, and a freeze doesn't affect your credit score or your existing accounts.

Before you apply for credit

Don't forget to unfreeze your credit first. A lender can't check a frozen credit report, so an application can be held up or declined until you lift the freeze. Ask the lender which bureau it uses and lift only that one, or lift all three if you aren't sure. If you're applying online, the application often names the bureau. A temporary lift turns the freeze back on by itself when it ends.
